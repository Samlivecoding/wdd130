Abuja

The Abuja City Gate, completed in 1986, serves as a symbolic and architectural gateway to Nigeria’s capital, reflecting the city’s modernity, unity, and cultural heritage. The Abuja City Gate was conceived in the early 1980s when the Nigerian government decided to relocate the federal capital from Lagos to Abuja, officially declared in 1981
